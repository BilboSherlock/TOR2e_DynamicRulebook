### Session 1: Another Unexpected Party
Trewsday 9th July 2965 T.A.

There may be nowhere better in Middle-Earth to celebrate than The Shire. And to truly party, you visit Buckland. And thus it was decided to host Northus the Blacksmith's birthday party in the town of Buckleberry, in the shade of Brandy Hall. 

Bror and Rhadam busy themselves with party plans, determined to celebrate the Dwarf's birthday properly. 


---

Lexis and Mars are travelling along the Great East Road, trailing alongside the Old Forest to the left.

There is a shadowy, looming presence in the wood, mars noticing and hopping down from the hedge beside the forest.

Northus is travelling with Balin and Ori towards Buckleberry. Northus is guided up from Buckleberry Ferry and encounters a small fence and curtain. Balin urges Northus to enter first, resulting ain a large crowd cheering on his arrival

Rhadam and Northus Drinking competition

Lexis sneaks up towards the party and Mars is on the fence. Bror mistakes Mars for a "Hairy Sausage" and grabs her from the fence. Lexis jumps clean over the fence to help her familiar. Bror still preoccupied with food doesn't notice for a moment

Bror learns Mars in not in fact a hairy sausage, but instead a cat. A can't that is in fact older than the dwarf, 

Bror and Lexis help each other moving the cake out of the marquee. The cake is fantastic but is cold. Gandalf arrives to heat up the now cold buffet

Everyone digs in except Lexis. Gilda Goodheart offers Lexis a salad. Gilda Goodheart receiving the half carved statuette.

A cold unnatural fog rolls in and takes Gilda Goodheart, pairs of light are Barrow-Whites eyes.

# Session 2

#### Evening

The company meet Gandalf and Old Broadbelt and discuss the events of the night, discovering it is a barrow wight's work. Gilda Goodheart, and others, were taken by the spirits in the mist. They do not have long, Mars has pinpointed Gilda's general location based on scent, and it is time to rescue the Hobbits.

Discuss plans with Gandalf and Old Broadbelt.

Lexis recounts what she knows of Barrow-Wites.

Mars picks up Gilda's scent and pins it on the map.

Lots of food served, Bror dual welding sausages while Northus and Rhadam are still pretty drunk.

Rhadam challenges Lexis to a drinking contest and Lexis bosses it

#### Morning

But first Northus must convince Old Broadbelt to give him a chair, specifically in this case it turns out to be a family heirloom, to make flaming torches out of them. Despite being offered premade torches as an alternative.

Breaks some chairs.

Begin the journey south.

Old Broadbelt gifts each some Pipeweed of varying types to everyone.

# Session 3: Wargs in Buckland

The company travels south from Buckleberry for a short time, before the sun has risen. Northus believes he has found a short cut and splits from the path, Lexis, Bror and Mars folling, Leaving Rhadam continuing the originally proposed route. 

Soon after, when stopping to rest and gather bearings, all are come upon by wargs from the direction of the Old Forest.

4 gather by the main group while 2 attack Rhadam who is alone over the hillside.

Bror facing the one eyed Warg faced down previously, 

Northus was facing a Warg with a aggressive in mouth

Lexis shoots and kills a Warg instantly with a piercing blow, another bounds between rocks, eyes locked on hers

Rhadam facing down a burnt Warg and another, cutting the tail from the second and dropping his helm

# Session 4: Old foes gone and new Allies gained
#### The Fight

Rhadam wounded (3 days - shoulder warg fangs bite)

Rhadam kills Burnt Beast (cinematic)

Bror Kills warg - ancestors (magical success)

Northus Kills warg - dagger

Lexis kills warg impales with arrow on tree

#### Finding Rhadam

Rhadam sounds the kings horn

Lexis, Bror and Northus race to rhadam, following mars lead

Lexis and Bror find rhadam and believe him to be dead. Bror begins to wail and cry, mouning and grieving where Lexis delivers first aid and quickly realises Rhadam is alive though wounded and exhausted

Rhadam urges Lexis to go along with it and she does so, tending to his shoulder wound.

#### Meeting Rori

Near the site Northus goes in another direction and meets rorimac brandybuck, by punching him in the face and breaking his nose

Northus and Rori race along the road, hearing bror’s wailing cries Northus picks up Rori and sprints even faster, only to crest the hill and see the dead wargs and Rhadam on the floor, bror by his side loudly grieving.

#### New Friends

Rori invites the company back to his home, where he tells them about the old forest and him and his sisters experience there.

Rori asks to join the company, and explains his use.

Rhadam rests up and now only has 1 day of recovery from his shoulder wound

Northus requires more homemade torches, which Rori gives the option of a stool or an heirloom. On a roll the stool is chosen. And Lexis breaks the chair apart into three sticks.

#### The Dwarven Craft

A local blacksmith is honoured to help out the party, sharpening axes, blades, and weapons.

Cat armour is requested, a double sided axe, and slingshot, to be built upon return.

The crimson dagger has a chain to be attached, the dwarves all help each other with the crafting of the chain and blade. The blacksmith is in awe.

Set off on the road to Haysend.
