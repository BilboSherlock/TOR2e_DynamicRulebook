#### Breredon
The peoples of Breredon are concerned as the Withywindle has dropped with depth noticeably since the night prior.

>"We often have logs and branches clog the river but nothing quite like this."

They are weary of the Adventurers, concerned of luring worse things from the forest by angering it. They can provide a boat for the Withywindle if persuaded.
